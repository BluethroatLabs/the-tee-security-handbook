# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Security engineers, protocol builders, researchers, and readers evaluating
trusted execution environments. They use the handbook to learn, verify claims,
and identify the people responsible for its guidance.

## Product Purpose

Make TEE security knowledge practical, inspectable, and openly attributable.
Success means readers can understand the material, trust its provenance, and
find the people who maintain it.

## Brand Personality

Technical, candid, and human. The handbook should feel authored by security
practitioners rather than presented by a faceless documentation platform.

## Anti-references

Avoid oversized dashboard heroes, generic SaaS metric panels, anonymous
initial-only profiles, decorative complexity, and contribution rankings that
imply line count equals value.

## Design Principles

- Put people before metrics.
- Make provenance inspectable without turning attribution into a competition.
- Keep dense technical information compact and legible.
- Explain what every contribution measure does and does not mean.
- Preserve the handbook's direct, practitioner-led voice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, keyboard-accessible links, descriptive image
alternatives, non-color status cues, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts,
and reduced-motion support.
